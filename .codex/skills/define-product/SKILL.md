# Define product

Use when product vision must become a PRD. Inputs: vision, stakeholders, constraints, `AGENTS.md`, and product indexes. Define problem, users/personas, jobs/outcomes, journeys, principles, stable requirements, MVP/later/out-of-scope, measurable success/release criteria, assumptions, risks, dependencies, and open decisions. Avoid implementation details except constraints and do not copy competitor features without need. Output `docs/product/prd.md`. Validate stable IDs, traceability, testability, explicit exclusions, and unresolved decisions. Stop for conflicting goals, legal guarantees, or decisions only a human can make. Make no unrelated changes.
